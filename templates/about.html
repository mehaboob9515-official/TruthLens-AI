🛡️ About TruthLens AI

TruthLens AI is an AI-powered news verification platform. It helps users identify whether news articles are Real or Fake using Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing.


🚀 Main Features

  • AI Fake News Detection
  • URL News Analysis
  • AI Article Summary
  • Trust Score
  • Evidence Verification
  • Source Credibility
  • Sentiment Analysis
  • Dashboard & Charts
  • History & PDF Export


🔍 How AI News Verification Works

TruthLens AI analyzes news content using Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing techniques. The system examines the available article content and produces multiple signals that can help users evaluate the reliability of a news story.

The analysis may consider the language used in the article, sentiment, source information, extracted evidence, and other available signals before generating the final result.


📊 Trust Score

The Trust Score is an indicator generated from the signals available to the TruthLens AI analysis system. It helps users understand how strongly the available evidence and analysis support the reliability of an article.

A Trust Score should not be interpreted as an absolute guarantee that an article is true or false. Users should consider the original source and supporting evidence before making important decisions.


😊 Sentiment Analysis

TruthLens AI analyzes the emotional tone and language used in news content. Sentiment analysis can help identify strongly positive, negative, or emotionally charged language.

Highly emotional or sensational language does not automatically mean that a story is false, but it can be a useful signal for further investigation.


🔎 Evidence Verification

TruthLens AI examines available evidence and verification signals associated with the analyzed article. These signals can help users identify claims that may require additional checking.

Whenever possible, users should compare important claims with reliable primary sources and independent reporting.


⚠️ Limitations of AI Verification

Artificial Intelligence can make mistakes. News articles may contain incomplete information, changing facts, misleading claims, or evidence that is difficult to automatically verify.

TruthLens AI is therefore designed as a supporting verification tool, not as a replacement for human judgment, reliable sources, or professional fact-checking.
